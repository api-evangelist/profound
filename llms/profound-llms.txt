# Profound

> Profound is a marketing platform for the AI era and a leading platform for Answer Engine Optimization (AEO). It helps brands measure and improve how they appear across AI answer engines and assistants — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Copilot, and Grok — through answer-engine insights, agent analytics, prompt volumes, and content optimization. Operated by Cooper Square Technologies Inc. (dba Profound), New York, NY. A programmatic "Profound External API" and an "Agent Analytics Ingestion API" are available (API access is included on the Enterprise plan).
